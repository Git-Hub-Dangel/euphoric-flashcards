# Euphoric Flashcards

A free, offline, and private spaced-repetition flashcard plugin purpose built for effective language learning. Built on autodidactic learning experience to be the daily core of your learning system. Review flashcards and actively solidify them with sentence building practice and a suite of additional features. Initially based off the [OSR](https://github.com/st3v3nmw/obsidian-spaced-repetition) plugin.

**Euphoric enriches learning with features such as**

* a flexible, dynamic syntax that captures more than just words
   * word-type categorisation (noun, adjective, verbs, or custom types)
   * embed usage examples and sentences right within the flashcard
   * embed explanations or notes right within the flashcard
        * supports transliterations of glyphic languages or pronunciations, e.g. `汉语 - hànyǔ - Chinese`
* learn mode (alongside 3 other review modes)
    * smart sentence-building exercises
* native two-way card reviews
* load-balancing
* the Open Spaced Repetition scheduling algorithm
* and many more extensive features for night owls, power language learners, configuration lovers, minimalists, and those who want to import and use their existing Obsidian flashcards with this system

**What is Euphoric for?**

First of all, Cześć! I'm Daniel. I speak six languages, 5 of 'em fluently, 1 in intermediate proficiency and am currently learning two more in concurrence. I have been learning languages by myself as a sidekick since about four years now and just about for an hour a day. I have created this tiny, fun and effective system to apprehend a richness in both grammar and vocabulary rapidly.

This plugin, instead of solely being a mundane repository for your vocabulary, forms the backbone of your learning and unifies raw input and grammatical concepts in a feature suite that gives you the leverage to understand and apply (not simply memorize) your words. At its core it's still a flashcard plugin. But one, that dynamically adjusts to your distinct type of note taking and actively practises with you. It is meant to be the core of [a small coherent learning system](https://www.jrdk.de/notes/linguistic-learning-system).
